# Habsida Hackathon 2026 notes

## Rules
- Team competition, 3–5 people, startup focus (validate hypotheses, build product, pitch).
- Main focus must be Korea.
- Prizes: 1st 1,500,000 KRW, 2nd 700,000 KRW, 3rd 300,000 KRW.

## Judging criteria
- Problem: relevant and clearly defined? Why important?
- Solution: does it address the problem? Prototype or demo?
- Prospect: will people use it? Potential for further development?
- Presentation: clear and convincing? How well did we answer questions?

## Pitch outline
1. Hook: "Remember Purple Place? Now the cake you build is the cake you get."
2. Problem: fixed menus, messy DMs, people don't know what they want, dietary needs ignored
3. Validation: survey numbers and bakery quotes
4. Solution: kitchen builder + AI suggestions + marketplace of small bakeries
5. Live demo (judge scans QR, order appears on bakery dashboard)
6. Competitors and our difference (Adori, bakery DMs, delivery apps)
7. Business model: commission per order, featured listings, licensing to chains
8. Marketing and growth: nostalgia, viral "virtual vs real", students, then chains
9. Team
10. Closing line

## Judge Q&A
- Who bakes? Partner bakeries; we're a marketplace.
- How do you make money? Commission (10–15%), then featured listings and licensing.
- Why would bakeries join? Free, more orders, no DMs, new customers.
- Real cake looks different? Builder only offers what the bakery can make; photo before delivery.
- Allergic reaction? Rule-based filters (not AI), verified badges, required allergen fields, cross-contact warning.
- Why no signup? Every step loses customers; phone number is enough.
