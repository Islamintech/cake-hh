# Competitor research (checked Sep 24, 2026)

Searched in English, Russian and Uzbek. No one found in Uzbekistan doing a game-like cake builder + marketplace of many bakeries.

## Uzbekistan: single bakeries taking custom orders
- Leamor (leamor.uz): customers choose look and ingredients or suggest their own design; one bakery's order form, not a visual builder.
- Lokko (lokko.uz): cakes of any shape, orders by phone, delivery in Tashkent and region.
- Merenga (merenga.uz): vegan, lactose-free and keto desserts, shows demand for dietary options.
- Tortlandiya (tortlandiya.uz): premium, minimum 3 kg orders.
- Also: Impreza, La Miranda.

## Uzbekistan: delivery apps (no builder)
- Nonbor Express: order food, bread and sweets from nearby shops and bakeries, real-time tracking.
- Nonbor: restaurants, cafés and home cooks.
- Tochka Eats: general restaurant delivery.
Possible partners or competitors for delivery if we expand to Uzbekistan.

## Closest competitor abroad
- Adori (Qatar): design-your-cake app (shape, flavor, size, decorations), ready-made designs, payment, delivery tracking. Differences: they bake themselves (not a marketplace) and it's a design tool, not a game.
- Russia: bakery-site "cake constructors" (choose filling, weight, shape, decor, attach a photo), basically forms.

## What it means for the pitch
- Demand is proven: people pay for custom cakes; Adori shows a design app can work.
- Our differences: game experience, marketplace of many small bakeries, dietary filters, AI suggestions.
- Uzbekistan = strong "next market" slide.
